Greentown Learning Privacy Policy

Greentown Learning 隐私政策与儿童个人信息保护规则

Effective / 生效日期: July 21, 2026 / 2026年7月21日

English 简体中文 Privacy choices and support / 隐私选择与支持

1. Scope and operator

This policy describes how the current Greentown Learning school beta processes information. Greentown Learning is operated for this beta by Aaron Cox. Questions and privacy requests may be sent to aaron.tcox@icloud.com.

The current beta is intended for students who receive an account from a participating school or authorized tester. It does not currently offer public self-registration, advertising, or subscriptions. This policy will be updated before an individual or family subscription service is released.

Children's use: Greentown Learning is designed for young learners. A school, parent, or legal guardian should authorize a child's use. In mainland China, personal information of a child under 14 should be processed only with consent from the child's parent or other legal guardian.

2. Information processed

Account and school information

  • Class code, username, PIN, and other credentials entered to authenticate a school account.
  • Student display name, student identifier, class identifier, class name, and school-assigned access settings.
  • A session token stored on the device so an authorized student can remain signed in.

Reading activity and progress

  • Books, units, modules, and levels opened or completed.
  • Read counts, completion status, unlock status, accuracy results, review status, and relevant timestamps.
  • Portal usage information such as the area used, start and end times, duration, and last screen.

Microphone, speech recognition, and Reading Submissions

  • The microphone is used only for speaking activities, automatic reading checks, and Reading Submissions after permission is granted.
  • For automatic checks, live audio may be processed by Apple's Speech service to produce recognized text. Depending on the device and Apple's service availability, audio may be processed by Apple on its servers. Greentown Learning uses the resulting text to evaluate the reading activity and does not intentionally store that live recognition audio as a Reading Submission.
  • For designated school Reading Submissions, the student chooses to record a reading. The audio file is saved locally until upload succeeds, then uploaded for review by authorized school staff. Submission status, teacher review status, and an optional teacher note may also be stored.

Downloaded content and technical information

  • Book manifests, page images, audio, curriculum maps, cached progress, and pending sync items may be stored locally on the device.
  • When the app connects to Supabase, Apple services, or an authorized school content server, those services may automatically receive limited network and diagnostic information such as IP address, request time, and error information.
  • When the beta is installed through TestFlight, Apple may process beta installation, crash, diagnostic, and tester feedback information under Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate students, deliver assigned reading content, apply teacher pacing and access rules, save and restore progress, perform reading checks, submit recordings for teacher review, provide usage and progress reports to authorized staff, retry offline actions, maintain security, troubleshoot problems, and respond to support or privacy requests.

4. When information is shared

Greentown Learning does not sell personal information and the current beta contains no third-party advertising.

Information may be disclosed only as needed to:

  • Participating schools and authorized staff: to administer accounts, control assignments and pacing, review Reading Submissions, and view progress or usage reports.
  • Supabase: to provide database, authentication-related backend functions, file storage, and content delivery used by the app.
  • Apple: to provide TestFlight/App Store services, device permissions, and speech recognition where used.
  • Authorized school infrastructure: to deliver school-local reading content when configured by the school.
  • Legal and safety purposes: when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect students, users, the service, or others.

5. Storage, international processing, and retention

Information may be stored on the student's device and in cloud systems used by Greentown Learning. Supabase and Apple may process information in countries or regions outside the user's location, including outside mainland China. Schools and guardians should consider this before authorizing use in mainland China. Any public mainland-China release will require additional consent, localization, data-transfer, and regulatory work.

Account, progress, usage, and submission information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the school service, support educational administration, resolve technical or security issues, meet school instructions, and comply with legal obligations. The current beta does not apply one universal automatic retention period to every participating school. Reading Submission audio may remain available to authorized staff until it is deleted through the service's administrative process.

Downloaded books, cached progress, pending sync data, and session information may remain on a device until the user signs out and local data is cleared, the app is removed, the school resets the device, or an authorized administrator removes the data.

6. Children's privacy

School accounts are created and managed by participating schools; children do not publicly register for the current beta. Schools and guardians are responsible for providing the authorization and notices required for student participation. Greentown Learning limits collection to information used for reading instruction, progress, classroom administration, security, and support.

For children under 14 in mainland China, a parent or other legal guardian must consent before personal information is processed. A guardian may request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent by contacting the participating school or the email address listed in this policy.

7. Privacy choices, correction, and deletion

The current school beta does not allow a student to create an account independently. A parent, guardian, student, or authorized school representative may request access, correction, deletion, or account deactivation through the school or by emailing aaron.tcox@icloud.com.

To protect a child, Greentown Learning may verify the requester's identity and authority with the school before changing or deleting a school-managed account. Data may be retained where required by law, necessary to protect security, or required under a valid school retention obligation. Do not send a student's PIN or a recording by email.

See the Privacy Choices and Support page for request instructions.

8. Security

Greentown Learning uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Schools and users should keep account credentials private and report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

9. AI-assisted illustrations

Some illustrations in Greentown Learning were created or enhanced using generative artificial intelligence and reviewed before publication. Student recordings and personal information are not used to generate these illustrations.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, its service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised. Material changes affecting children or the purpose, method, or scope of processing may require renewed school or guardian notice and consent.

一、适用范围与运营者

本政策说明当前 Greentown Learning 学校测试版如何处理信息。本测试版由 Aaron Cox 运营。有关隐私的问题或请求，请发送邮件至 aaron.tcox@icloud.com。

当前测试版仅供参与学校提供账号的学生或经授权的测试人员使用，目前不提供面向公众的自主注册、广告或订阅服务。在个人或家庭订阅服务上线前，本政策将进行更新。

儿童使用说明：Greentown Learning 面向低龄学习者。儿童使用本应用前，应当获得学校、父母或其他法定监护人的授权。在中国大陆，处理不满十四周岁儿童的个人信息，应当取得其父母或者其他监护人的同意。

二、我们处理的信息

账号与学校信息

  • 用于登录学校账号的班级代码、用户名、PIN 码及其他认证信息。
  • 学生显示姓名、学生标识符、班级标识符、班级名称，以及学校分配的访问和进度设置。
  • 为使已授权学生保持登录状态而存储在设备上的会话令牌。

阅读活动与学习进度

  • 打开或完成的图书、单元、模块和级别。
  • 阅读次数、完成状态、解锁状态、准确率结果、审核状态及相关时间。
  • 功能区域使用信息，例如使用的区域、开始和结束时间、使用时长及最后停留页面。

麦克风、语音识别与 Reading Submission（阅读提交）

  • 只有在获得权限后，麦克风才会用于口语活动、自动阅读检查和 Reading Submission。
  • 进行自动阅读检查时，实时音频可能由 Apple 的语音识别服务处理并转换为文字。根据设备和 Apple 服务的可用情况，音频可能由 Apple 服务器处理。Greentown Learning 使用识别结果评估阅读活动，不会有意将该实时识别音频作为 Reading Submission 保存。
  • 对于学校指定的 Reading Submission，学生主动录制阅读音频。音频文件会在设备上保存至上传成功，然后上传供获授权的学校工作人员审核。系统也可能保存提交状态、教师审核状态和可选的教师备注。

下载内容与技术信息

  • 图书清单、页面图片、音频、课程地图、缓存进度和待同步项目可能存储在设备本地。
  • 当应用连接 Supabase、Apple 服务或学校授权的内容服务器时，这些服务可能自动接收有限的网络和诊断信息，例如 IP 地址、请求时间和错误信息。
  • 通过 TestFlight 安装测试版时，Apple 可能依据其自身条款和隐私政策处理测试版安装、崩溃、诊断和测试反馈信息。

三、信息使用目的

我们使用相关信息来验证学生身份、提供已分配的阅读内容、执行教师设置的进度和访问规则、保存及恢复学习进度、进行阅读检查、向教师提交录音、向获授权工作人员提供学习进度和使用报告、重试离线操作、维护安全、排查问题，以及处理支持或隐私请求。

四、信息共享情形

Greentown Learning 不出售个人信息，当前测试版不包含第三方广告。

仅在提供服务所必需的范围内，信息可能提供给：

  • 参与学校及获授权工作人员：用于管理账号、控制作业与进度、审核 Reading Submission，以及查看学习进度或使用报告。
  • Supabase：用于提供应用所需的数据库、认证相关后端功能、文件存储和内容分发。
  • Apple：用于提供 TestFlight/App Store 服务、系统权限，以及应用使用的语音识别服务。
  • 学校授权的基础设施：在学校配置本地内容来源时，用于提供校内阅读内容。
  • 法律与安全目的：法律要求或为保护学生、用户、本服务或他人而合理必要时。

五、存储地点、跨境处理与保存期限

信息可能存储在学生设备以及 Greentown Learning 使用的云端系统中。Supabase 和 Apple 可能在用户所在国家或地区以外（包括中国大陆以外）处理信息。学校和监护人在授权中国大陆用户使用前应当考虑这一情况。面向中国大陆的正式公开版本仍需完成额外的同意、本地化、数据跨境和监管合规工作。

账号、学习进度、使用情况和提交信息仅在合理必要的期限内保存，以提供学校服务、支持教学管理、处理技术或安全问题、执行学校指示并履行法律义务。当前测试版不会对所有参与学校统一设置一个固定的自动保存期限。Reading Submission 音频可能在通过服务的管理流程删除前继续供获授权工作人员查看。

已下载图书、缓存进度、待同步数据和会话信息可能保留在设备上，直至用户退出并清除本地数据、卸载应用、学校重置设备，或获授权管理员删除相关数据。

六、儿童个人信息保护规则

学校账号由参与学校创建和管理，儿童不能在当前测试版中面向公众自行注册。学校和监护人应当为学生参与提供所需授权和告知。Greentown Learning 仅处理阅读教学、学习进度、课堂管理、安全和支持所需的信息。

在中国大陆，处理不满十四周岁儿童的个人信息，应当事先取得其父母或其他法定监护人的同意。监护人可通过参与学校或本政策所列邮箱，请求查阅、更正、删除相关信息或撤回同意。

七、隐私选择、更正与删除

当前学校测试版不允许学生独立创建账号。父母、监护人、学生或获授权的学校代表可通过学校，或发送邮件至 aaron.tcox@icloud.com，请求查阅、更正、删除信息或停用账号。

为保护儿童安全，Greentown Learning 在更改或删除学校管理的账号前，可能需要通过学校核实请求人的身份和权限。法律要求、安全保护或有效的学校保存义务所必需的信息可能继续保留。请勿通过电子邮件发送学生 PIN 码或录音文件。

具体请求方式请参阅隐私选择与支持页面。

八、安全措施

Greentown Learning 采取旨在保护信息的管理、技术和组织措施，但任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。学校和用户应妥善保管账号信息，并及时报告疑似未经授权的访问。

九、AI 辅助插图说明

Greentown Learning 中的部分插图由生成式人工智能创作或辅助制作，并在发布前经过审核。学生录音和个人信息不会被用于生成这些插图。

十、政策更新

当应用、服务提供商或法律要求发生变化时，本政策可能更新，页面顶部的生效日期也会相应修改。若涉及儿童信息处理目的、方式或范围的重大变化，可能需要重新向学校或监护人告知并取得同意。
