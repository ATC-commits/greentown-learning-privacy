Individual accounts

One individual subscription covers one learner and both Readers Road and Class Reading. In My curricula, choose a map and select Download curriculum. Open a downloaded version to read it. Return to My curricula to switch maps; each version keeps its own progress. Free Reading and teacher recording submissions are available only through eligible school accounts.

Automatic checks compare recognized words with the book on your device. We sync the result, not your recording or recognized words. Speech recognition may require an internet connection and a supported speech service even when the book is downloaded.

Subscriptions, downloads and access

Where offered, individual plans cost US $4.99 per month or $39.99 per year. Apple shows the price for your storefront before purchase. In the iPad app, open Grown-ups: account and subscription to buy, restore purchases or manage renewal. Use the same individual account when restoring. Canceling renewal preserves access until the paid period ends.

A temporary connection or service error does not immediately stop previously verified downloaded reading. Reconnect and choose Check access and refresh. The offline allowance ends no later than seven days after the last successful check or 72 hours after the known paid period ends, whichever comes first. Confirmed expiry or refund pauses paid reading, including downloads. Progress is kept. On the website, keep the reading tab open when offline. A new browser session may need a connection to load the portal or verify access.

To delete an individual account and its progress, open account settings, choose Delete individual account and confirm with the adult password. Cancel any Apple subscription separately. For help, email the account email address and a description of the problem; never send a password, PIN, recording or payment card number.

Schools and regional availability

Schools can request a class subscription. Website checkout is not available. The native app is not offered through the public mainland China App Store. Any individual service offered there is browser-only.

Technical support

For login, download, audio, speech-recognition, recording, or progress-sync problems, email the address above. Include the device model, iOS/iPadOS version, app version, approximate time of the problem, and a description of what happened. Do not include a student's PIN.

个人账号

一份订阅供一名学习者使用，包含 Readers Road 和 Class Reading。在“My curricula”中选择并下载课程地图，再打开已下载的版本。返回该列表可随时切换，各版本分别保存进度。个人账号不包含 Free Reading，也不向教师提交录音。

设备将识别出的文字与书本文本进行比较。我们同步阅读结果，不上传录音或识别文字。即使已下载书籍，语音识别仍可能需要联网并使用受支持的语音服务。

在已开放的地区，美国价格为每月 4.99 美元或每年 39.99 美元；购买前以 Apple 显示的当地价格为准。在 iPad 的成人账号与订阅设置中可购买、恢复购买或管理续订。取消续订后可使用至已付费期限结束。

暂时的网络或服务故障不会立即中断已验证的离线阅读。请联网并重新检查访问权限。离线期限最迟为上次成功验证后七天，或已知付费期限结束后七十二小时，以较早者为准。确认到期或退款后，已下载的付费阅读也会暂停，但进度保留。离线时请保持网页打开；新的浏览器会话可能需要联网加载网页或验证权限。

成人可在账号设置中选择删除个人账号，并输入密码确认。删除后学习进度不可恢复；请另行取消 Apple 订阅。请勿通过邮件发送密码、PIN 码、录音或银行卡号。
